## Overview

GridPINN is a physics-informed neural network (PINN) that predicts generator dispatch and bus voltages for AC optimal power flow (AC-OPF). The training loss includes power-balance and constraint terms, so predictions are pushed toward physically feasible operating points.

## Problem statement

AC-OPF chooses generator outputs and voltages that minimise generation cost while satisfying the AC power flow equations and operating limits. Solving it exactly is a nonconvex optimisation problem, and it must be re-solved whenever the load changes.

$$
\min_{P_g,\,Q_g,\,V,\,\theta} \; \sum_{i \in \mathcal{G}} c_i(P_{g,i})
$$

subject to the power-balance equations at every bus $i$:

$$
P_{g,i} - P_{d,i} = V_i \sum_{j} V_j \left( G_{ij}\cos\theta_{ij} + B_{ij}\sin\theta_{ij} \right)
$$

$$
Q_{g,i} - Q_{d,i} = V_i \sum_{j} V_j \left( G_{ij}\sin\theta_{ij} - B_{ij}\cos\theta_{ij} \right)
$$

together with generator limits, voltage magnitude limits and line-flow limits.

## Motivation

A trained network can produce a dispatch in a single forward pass. The open question is whether those predictions stay physically feasible, which is what the physics terms in the loss are meant to improve.

## Approach

The model maps load conditions to generator dispatch and bus voltages. The loss combines supervised fit with physics penalties:

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{data}} + \lambda_{1}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{balance}} + \lambda_{2}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{limits}}
$$

- $\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}$: error against reference AC-OPF solutions.
- $\mathcal{L}_{\text{balance}}$: residual of the power-balance equations.
- $\mathcal{L}_{\text{limits}}$: penalty for violating generator and voltage limits.

## Limitations

- Penalty-based physics terms reduce constraint violations but do not guarantee feasibility.
- Results will be reported only on the test systems listed above and may not transfer to larger networks.
